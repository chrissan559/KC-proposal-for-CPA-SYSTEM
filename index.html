Common Questions

What You're Probably Wondering

How do I know the numbers are real?
Every deposit is confirmed by the trading platform (AffStore), not by us. You'll have access to their dashboard which shows registrations and first deposits independently. We don't control what they report.
What if the ads don't perform?
Ad performance is never guaranteed — that's the nature of paid traffic. What we control is everything after the click: the bot, the follow-up, the conversion rate. We optimize weekly to improve results. If a campaign isn't working, we kill it and test a different approach.
Why do you need 40%?
We built the entire technical system — the bot, the tracking infrastructure, the ad setup, the content, the optimization process. We absorb that cost and take 40% of revenue as the operating fee. You take 60% without lifting a finger.
What's the minimum budget to see real results?
$300–500 USD minimum to get statistically meaningful data. Below that, the ad platform doesn't have enough to learn from. $500–1000 is the sweet spot for a proper first test with reliable data.
Can I see results before committing more?
Yes. Start with a test budget, review the data after 2 weeks, then decide whether to scale. Nothing is locked in. This is a performance relationship — if the numbers work for you, we both win by doing more.
